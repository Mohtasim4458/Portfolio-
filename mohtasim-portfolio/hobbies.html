Online Game – Free Fire

Free Fire is an online battle royale game. Players compete against each other, collect equipment and try to survive until the end. It is enjoyable because it requires teamwork, planning and quick decisions.

Physical Game – Cricket

Cricket is a team sport played with a bat and ball. Two teams take turns batting and fielding. I enjoy cricket because it improves teamwork, focus and physical activity.
